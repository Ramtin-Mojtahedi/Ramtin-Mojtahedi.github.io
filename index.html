Research focus

Four connected areas of clinical intelligence.

Reliable AI for understanding, prediction, and decision support—without losing sight of clinical context.

01

Lung transplantation & EVLP

Multimodal and longitudinal models for donor-lung assessment, transplant outcomes, ex vivo lung perfusion, and individualized clinical risk.

02

Medical imaging & cancer AI

2D/3D segmentation, classification, radiomics, and treatment-response modelling for liver, abdominal, chest, and cardiovascular imaging.

03

Foundation models & efficient learning

Vision transformers, SAM/MedSAM adaptation, self-supervised and contrastive learning, few-shot learning, and parameter-efficient tuning.

04

Trustworthy clinical machine learning

Nested validation, external testing, calibration, uncertainty, interpretability, subgroup analysis, and reproducible pipelines.
